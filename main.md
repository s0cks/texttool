# Lumumba

In September 1960, Central Intelligence Agency (‍CIA‍) Station Chief Larry Devlin received a top-secret cable in Léopoldville introducing a specialized operative known only as “Joe from Paris” at the time.
This emissary would later get identified as Dr. Sidney Gottlieb, chief chemist of the agency’s clandestine mind-control project, MK-ULTRA.
Equipped with a bio-chemical kit prepared at Fort Detrick, Gottlieb became tasked with assassinating Patrice Lumumba, the democratically elected Prime Minister of the newly independent Republic of the Congo.
The plot called for lacing Lumumba’s food or toothpaste with a lethal biological pathogen, an action authorized under the doctrine of plausible deniability at the highest levels of the Eisenhower administration.

Concurrently, under sub-projects such as MKDELTA, MKSEARCH, and MKOFTEN, Gottlieb’s researchers aggressively investigated Central Nervous System compounds, specifically testing analogs of DOPA, dopamine, picrotoxin, and potent hallucinogenic alkaloids like Ibogaine.
Motivated by the operational goal of behavior modification, the agency synthesized pharmacological agents capable of destabilizing human consciousness, inducing disorientation, memory erasure, and transient physical paralysis. Subjects exposed to these non-consensual trials experienced terrifying waking hallucinations, profound psychological disintegration, and persistent systemic trauma.
